Pizza Recipe

Description

Creamy and mildly spiced tomato curry sauce is an excellent stand-in for marinara sauce. This recipe uses tikka masala sauce,
but you could use any flavor of Indian-style simmer sauce in its place. Thinly slice the potatoes using a mandoline
slicer to ensure they cook through by the time the pizza crust is ready.

Ingrediants

  • 3 teaspoons vegetable oil
  • 1 pound fresh or thawed frozen pizza dough
  • 1 medium red potato, halved lengthwise, then sliced 1/8-inch thick
  • 2/3 cup jarred tikka masala sauce, plus more for serving
  • 1 cup lightly packed baby spinach
  • 1 cup shredded cooked chicken
  • 1/4 cup thinly sliced red onion
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • chopped fresh cilantro, for garnish

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 425 degrees F (220 degrees C). Brush 2 teaspoons oil over a 12-inch pizza pan (or for a larger, thinner-crust pizza, use a 14-inch pizza
    pan or 9x13-inch baking sheet).

  2. On a lightly floured work surface, roll dough into a 12-inch circle; transfer to the prepared pan.
    Bake crust until just starting to brown, about 10 minutes.

  3. Meanwhile, place potato slices in a microwave-safe dish; toss with remaining 1 teaspoon oil. Cover with plastic wrap,
    venting slightly. Microwave on high until potatoes are just tender, stirring halfway through, about 4 minutes.

  4. Spread tikka masala sauce over parbaked crust, leaving a 1-inch border. Top with potato slices, shingling them to fit, then top with spinach, chicken, and red onion.
    Sprinkle with mozzarella.

  5. Bake until crust is golden and cheese is bubbly, 10 to 15 minutes. Let stand 10 minutes, then cut into 8 slices. Garnish with cilantro.
    If you like, serve with additional tikka masala sauce, warmed, for dipping or drizzling.
